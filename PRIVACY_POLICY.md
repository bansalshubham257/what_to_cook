# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 1, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to What To Cook - AI Meal Planner ("we", "our", "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## 2. Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Account Information**: Email address, phone number, password (hashed), name
- **Profile Data**: Dietary preferences, cuisine preferences, household size
- **Firebase Authentication UID** (if you sign in with Google/Apple)

### Usage Data
- **Meal Plans**: Recipes you save, meals you schedule, "made today" marks
- **Kitchen Inventory**: Ingredients you add, quantities, expiration tracking
- **Shopping Lists**: Items you add, quantities, categories
- **Notes & Checklists**: Personal notes, recipe details, images you upload
- **Favorites**: Recipes you mark as favorites

### Technical Data
- **Device Information**: Device model, OS version, app version
- **Analytics**: Feature usage, crash reports, performance metrics (via Sentry if enabled)
- **Advertising ID**: For AdMob personalized ads (Google's advertising identifier)

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- **Provide Core Features**: Meal planning, recipe discovery, kitchen management, shopping lists
- **Personalize Experience**: Recommend recipes based on your preferences and history
- **Sync Across Devices**: Cloud backup of your data via Firebase/Auth
- **Improve App**: Analytics, crash reporting, feature optimization
- **Advertising**: Show relevant ads via Google AdMob (uses Advertising ID)
- **Communication**: Important updates, security notices (no marketing emails without consent)

## 4. Data Sharing

We **do not sell** your personal information. We share data only:

- **Service Providers**: Firebase (auth/database), Google AdMob (ads), Sentry (error tracking)
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law, court order, or government request
- **Business Transfers**: In case of merger, acquisition, or asset sale

## 5. Data Storage & Security

- **Database**: PostgreSQL on Railway (encrypted at rest and in transit)
- **Authentication**: Firebase Auth (industry-standard OAuth2/OIDC)
- **Passwords**: Bcrypt hashed, never stored in plain text
- **Images**: Stored locally on device; optional cloud sync via Firebase Storage
- **API**: HTTPS/TLS 1.2+ for all network communication

## 6. Your Rights

You can:
- **Access** your data via the app (Profile → Export Data)
- **Rectify** incorrect information in Profile settings
- **Delete** your account and all data (Profile → Delete Account)
- **Opt-out** of personalized ads (Android Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out)
- **Withdraw Consent** for analytics (contact support)

## 7. Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in EU). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us for immediate deletion.

## 8. International Transfers

Your data may be processed on servers in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate. We ensure adequate safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses, Privacy Shield framework).

## 9. Retention

- **Active Accounts**: Data retained while account exists
- **Deleted Accounts**: Data purged within 30 days (backups up to 90 days)
- **Analytics**: Aggregated/anonymous data retained indefinitely

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified via in-app notice or email. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
